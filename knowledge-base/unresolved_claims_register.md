# M2.1E — Unresolved Claims Register: Uttarakhand Tenancy Act, 2021

This register records every material unresolved legal or implementation question identified during the research phase for the Uttarakhand-only legal corpus (M2.1E). No claim is marked resolved unless supporting evidence is actually recorded.

## Claims

### Claim 1: Functional Status of Rent Authorities
- **Claim ID**: UK-UC-001
- **Description**: The functional status of Rent Authorities appointed under Section 30 of the Uttarakhand Tenancy Act, 2021 is unverified per district. While the Act provides that the District Collector or District Magistrate may appoint an officer not below the rank of Deputy Collector as Rent Authority, no verification has been conducted as to whether such appointments have been made or whether the authorities are functionally operational.
- **Why it matters**: Tenants and landlords relying on statutory remedies for security deposit disputes need to know whether the Rent Authority in their district is actually functional. Legal commencement (01.01.2023) does not guarantee operational functionality.
- **Current evidence**: Section 30 of the Act identifies the appointment power; the implementation report notes that "the functional status of Rent Authorities, Rent Courts, and Rent Tribunals in Uttarakhand requires further verification."
- **Missing evidence**: District-wise verification of Rent Authority appointments and functional status; official gazette notifications of appointments.
- **Risk if answered incorrectly**: Tenants may erroneously believe they can access statutory dispute resolution mechanisms that are not actually available; landlords may incorrectly assume jurisdictional authority over disputes.
- **Blocks prototype**: Yes - the prototype's ability to route deposit dispute cases to the appropriate forum depends on knowing which authorities are functional.
- **Status**: open

### Claim 2: Confirmed Rule Text under Section 47
- **Claim ID**: UK-UC-002
- **Description**: The rule-making rules under Section 47 of the Uttarakhand Tenancy Act, 2021 have not been fully verified/confirmed from official sources. While Section 47 provides that "The State Government may make rules subject to prior publication," no confirmed rule text was found during this audit.
- **Why it matters**: The operational functioning of the Act may depend on these rules - they govern procedures, evidence standards, and enforcement mechanisms for deposit disputes, refund claims, and authority operations.
- **Current evidence**: Section 47 text is verified from the Gazette (SRC-02); the implementation report notes that "Rule-making rules have not been fully verified/confirmed from official sources" and that "no confirmed rule text found as of the audit date."
- **Missing evidence**: Confirmed rule text, published rule-making notifications, or a statement from the Uttarakhand State Government that no rules are currently necessary.
- **Risk if answered incorrectly**: The prototype may apply incorrect procedural or substantive rules to deposit dispute cases; users may receive inaccurate guidance on procedures.
- **Blocks prototype**: Partially - the prototype can reference the statutory provisions, but procedural guidance may be inaccurate.
- **Status**: open

### Claim 3: Rule-Making Notifications
- **Claim ID**: UK-UC-003
- **Description**: Gazette notifications for rule-making must be published prior to enforcement under Section 47, but no confirmed notifications were found during this audit. The requirement for prior publication is stated in the Act, but its actual implementation is unverified.
- **Why it matters**: Without confirmed rule-making notifications, the legal basis for certain procedural rules is uncertain, affecting the prototype's ability to provide accurate procedural guidance.
- **Current evidence**: The implementation report states: "Gazette notifications for rule-making must be published prior to enforcement - but no confirmed notifications found during this audit."
- **Missing evidence**: Copies of any published rule-making notifications in the Uttarakhand Official Gazette; a statement from the Uttarakhand Government confirming no rules are currently in force.
- **Risk if answered incorrectly**: The prototype may reference non-existent or procedural rules; users may be guided by incorrect procedural information.
- **Blocks prototype**: Minimal - the prototype can operate without procedural rule details, but accuracy is compromised.
- **Status**: open

### Claim 4: Exemption Categories under Section 3
- **Claim ID**: UK-UC-004
- **Description**: The exact categories of exemptions under Section 3 of the Uttarakhand Tenancy Act, 2021 have not been fully verified per property. While Section 3 states "Nothing in this Act shall apply to any [categories of premises exempted]," the specific categories and their applicability requires property-by-property verification.
- **Why it matters**: The prototype's applicability determinations depend on whether a property is exempt from the Act's provisions. Incorrect exemption determinations could lead to the prototype applying the Act to properties it doesn't cover, or failing to apply the Act to properties it does cover.
- **Current evidence**: Section 3 text is present in the Act (SRC-02), but the specific exemption categories and their scope require verification per property. The implementation report notes that exemptions "must be verified per property."
- **Missing evidence**: A comprehensive list of exemption categories under Section 3 with property-type classifications; official government guidance on exemption applicability.
- **Risk if answered incorrectly**: The prototype may incorrectly claim the Act applies to exempt properties, or fail to apply the Act to non-exempt properties; users may receive inaccurate applicability advice.
- **Blocks prototype**: Yes - the prototype's core functionality of determining whether the Act applies to a given property depends on resolving this claim.
- **Status**: open

### Claim 6: Commencement Applicability to Pre-Existing Deposits
- **Claim ID**: UK-UC-006
- **Description**: Whether the Uttarakhand Tenancy Act, 2021 applies to security deposits paid before the Act's commencement date of 01.01.2023, and to tenancies that commenced before commencement, is unresolved. The Act's Section 1(3) provides that it comes into force on a date appointed by the State Government, and different dates may be appointed for different provisions.
- **Why it matters**: Tenants who paid deposits in 2022 and landlords who collected them need to know whether the Act's deposit caps and refund provisions apply to their situation. The implementation report notes the legal commencement date but also warns that "Legal commencement does not guarantee operational functionality."
- **Current evidence**: Commencement notification No. 67/iv(2)-sh. Vi.-2023-53 (sha)/2021, dated 13 January 2023, with effective date 01.01.2023. Section 1(3) wording. The implementation report's distinction between legal commencement and administrative implementation.
- **Missing evidence**: Legal interpretation of whether Section 1(3) and Section 47 (savings) apply retrospectively or only to tenancies entered into after commencement; government circulars or judgments on the question.
- **Risk if answered incorrectly**: Tenants may incorrectly believe they have statutory protection they don't have, or conversely, waive rights they actually possess; landlords may incorrectly withhold or refund deposits.
- **Blocks prototype**: Yes - the prototype's determination of applicable provisions and expected behavior depends on resolving this claim.
- **Status**: open

### Claim 7: Rent Increase and Deposit Recalculation
- **Claim ID**: UK-UC-007
- **Description**: How rent increases during a tenancy affect the security deposit requirement under the Uttarakhand Tenancy Act, 2021 is unresolved. If the landlord increases the rent, must the tenant also increase the security deposit proportionally, and if so, at what rate?
- **Why it matters**: The prototype must be able to advise users on whether a rent increase triggers a deposit recalculation requirement, and if so, how the recalculation is computed.
- **Current evidence**: Section 11(1) specifies the deposit cap as a percentage of "rent," but does not explicitly address whether the cap and deposit amount must be recalculated when rent is revised under Section 9 or other provisions.
- **Missing evidence**: Government guidance, judicial interpretations, or the text of any rent revision rules under Section 47 regarding how deposit caps apply after rent revisions.
- **Risk if answered incorrectly**: The prototype may give incorrect advice about deposit obligations after rent increases; users may pay improperly increased deposits or fail to increase them when required.
- **Blocks prototype**: Partially - the prototype can reference the statutory deposit cap, but the rent increase interaction is unresolved.
- **Status**: open
