**User Query**: My landlord is refusing to refund my security deposit of 2 months rent after I vacated the residential premises. What is the expected outcome?

**Relevant Facts**: 
- Tenant vacated the premises on 2023-03-15
- Security deposit: 2 months rent (INR 20,000, rent = INR 10,000/month)

**Missing Facts**: Exact condition of premises at vacating, itemized deduction claims by landlord

**Applicable Provisions**: ST-UK-11(1) (deposit cap 2 months residential), ST-UK-11(2) (refund on vacant possession after liability deduction)

**Expected Behavior**: full_refund (unless landlord proves specific tenant liabilities deductible under S 11(2))

**Expected Answer**: full_refund if no valid deductions; partial_refund if landlord proves specific deductions for unpaid rent/damages

**Source Reference**: Uttarakhand Tenancy Act 2021, S 11(1)-(11(2)), SRC-02

**Applicability**: This case applies the standard residential deposit cap of 2 months rent under Uttarakhand law.
