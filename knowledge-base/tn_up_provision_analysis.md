# M2.1D.2 — TN/UP Provision Analysis

## Tamil Nadu

**Statute**: Tamil Nadu Regulation of Rights and Responsibilities of Landlords and Tenants Act, 2017 (Act 42 of 2017)

**Section 11(1)**: The landlord shall accept from the tenant a security deposit not exceeding three months' rent in case of residential premises and three months' rent in case of non-residential premises.

**Section 11(2)**: The security deposit shall be refunded to the tenant within such period as may be agreed upon between the landlord and the tenant in the tenancy agreement, or, in the absence of any such agreement, within a period of thirty days from the date of delivery of vacant possession to the landlord.

**Deposit limit**: 3 months (residential & non-residential), "save agreement to the contrary"
**Refund deadline**: 30 days after vacant possession if no agreement

**Source**: Tamil Nadu Act 42 of 2017 (SRC-03), https://www.tenancy.tn.gov.in/Home/AboutUs

## Uttar Pradesh

**Statute**: Uttar Pradesh Regulation of Urban Premises Tenancy Act, 2021 (U.P. Act No. 16 of 2021)

**Section 1(3)**: The State Government shall, by notification in the Official Gazette, specify the areas to which this Act shall apply. The Government may also extend the Act to other areas by a like notification.

**Section 1(4)**: Certain areas shall be deemed to have been included in the Act from 11 January 2021.

**Section 3**: The following shall be exempt from the operation of this Act: (categories of premises exempted).

**Section 11(1)**: The landlord and the tenant shall, before entering into the tenancy agreement, agree upon the amount of security deposit, which shall not exceed two months' rent in case of residential premises and six months' rent in case of non-residential premises.

**Section 11(2)**: The security deposit shall be refunded to the tenant on the date the landlord takes over vacant possession of the premises from the tenant, after making due deduction of any liability of the tenant. (Corrected from M2.1C claim of "within one month")

**Source**: U.P. Act No. 16 of 2021 (SRC-04), https://indiankanoon.org/doc/29855257/
