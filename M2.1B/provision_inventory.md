# M2.1B — Provision-Level Inventory: Indian Tenancy & Security-Deposit Law

**Scope:** Legal provisions relevant to tenant–landlord security-deposit refund disputes in India.
**Jurisdiction focus:** Central (India) + all States/UTs that have enacted tenancy legislation.
**Case type:** `security_deposit_dispute` (AI Lawyer M1/M2).
**Prepared:** 2026-11-06 (research date; verify against official sources before use).
**Status legend:** `current` = in force; `model` = model/central template, not automatically law; `uncertain` = not verified.

---

## A. Central (Union) statutes

### A1. Transfer of Property Act, 1882 (TPA) — Chapter V (Leases of Immovable Property)

| ID | Section | Title / Topic | Summary | Relevance to security-deposit disputes | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| TPA-105 | § 105 | Lease defined | Defines lease of immovable property for a certain time/rent. | Establishes the underlying lease relationship when no rent-control act applies. | current |
| TPA-106 | § 106 | Duration of certain leases; notice to quit | Month-to-month for non-agricultural leases; 15 days' written notice to terminate a month-to-month tenancy in absence of agreement. | Default notice period where agreement is silent; used to determine when tenant vacated and deposit became due. | current |
| TPA-107 | § 107 | Leases how made | Lease from year-to-year or >1 year must be registered; other leases may be oral/written. | Determines whether an unregistered long lease is admissible; affects proof of deposit terms. | current |
| TPA-108 | § 108 | Rights & liabilities of lessor/lessee | Lessor: deliver possession, disclose latent defects, quiet enjoyment, repairs; Lessee: pay rent, maintain, restore possession; lessor's remedies on breach. | Core duties (possession, repairs, restoration) that define lawful deductions from deposit. | current |
| TPA-109 | § 109 | Rights of lessor's transferee | Transferee of lessor's interest entitled to rent/conditions. | Relevant where property sold during tenancy; deposit obligation may pass. | current |
| TPA-111 | § 111 | Determination of lease | Modes by which lease ends (efflux of time, notice, forfeiture, surrender, merger, etc.). | Determines lawful end of tenancy and start of refund obligation. | current |
| TPA-112 | § 112 | Waiver of forfeiture | Forfeiture waived by lessor's acceptance of rent after forfeiture. | Affects eviction/forfeiture arguments that landlords raise to withhold deposit. | current |
| TPA-113 | § 113 | Waiver of notice to quit | Notice to quit may be waived by conduct. | Relevant to hold-over and deposit timing. | current |
| TPA-114 | § 114 | Relief against forfeiture for non-payment of rent | Court may relieve tenant from forfeiture on payment of arrears + costs. | Protects tenant from total forfeiture; limits landlord's ability to forfeit deposit. | current |
| TPA-114A | § 114A | Relief against forfeiture (other cases) | Relief where lease broken for non-rent breach, on equitable terms. | Limits forfeiture-based deposit withholding. | current |
| TPA-116 | § 116 | Effect of holding over | Tenant holding over with landlord's assent becomes tenant by sufferance/year-to-year per terms. | Determines rent/damages for overstay, a common deposit deduction. | current |

### A2. Indian Contract Act, 1872

| ID | Section | Title / Topic | Summary | Relevance | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| ICA-65 | § 65 | Obligation of person who has received advantage under void agreement | Restitution of benefit received. | Supports recovery of deposit where agreement void/unenforceable. | current |
| ICA-73 | § 73 | Compensation for loss/damage caused by breach | Damages for breach, including foreseeable losses. | Measures lawful deduction/compensation from deposit. | current |
| ICA-74 | § 74 | Compensation for breach where penalty stipulated | Reasonable compensation not exceeding stipulated amount; courts may reduce penalty. | Key for excessive deposit-forfeiture clauses. | current |
| ICA-75 | § 75 | Party rightfully rescinding contract entitled to compensation | Compensation on rescission. | Supports tenant claim where landlord repudiates. | current |
| ICA-56 | § 56 | Agreement to do impossible act / frustration | Void if impossible; subsequent impossibility discharges. | Rarely relevant (e.g., force majeure). | current |

### A3. Specific Relief Act, 1963

| ID | Section | Title / Topic | Summary | Relevance | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| SRA-5 | § 5 | Recovery of specific immovable property | Suit for possession by person entitled. | Tenant/landlord possession disputes. | current |
| SRA-6 | § 6 | Suit by person dispossessed without due process | Recovery of possession within 6 months. | Protects against illegal eviction; deposit claims often follow. | current |
| SRA-38 | § 38 | Perpetual injunction | Injunction to prevent breach. | Stops illegal utility cut-offs, withholding possession. | current |
| SRA-41 | § 41 | Injunction when refused | Limits injunctions; guides remedies. | Remedial context. | current |

### A4. Registration Act, 1908

| ID | Section | Title / Topic | Summary | Relevance | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| RA-17 | § 17 | Documents of which registration compulsory | Leases from year-to-year or >1 year (or reserving yearly rent) must be registered. | Unregistered long leases inadmissible as primary evidence; deposit terms may be unprovable. | current |
| RA-49 | § 49 | Effect of non-registration | Unregistered compulsorily registrable document cannot affect property or be received as evidence of transaction. | Evidentiary barrier in deposit disputes. | current |

### A5. Model Tenancy Act, 2021 (MTA) — *Model, not automatically law*

Adopted only where a State/UT has enacted it. Key provisions (section numbers per MoHUA model):

| ID | Section | Title / Topic | Summary | Relevance | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| MTA-1 | § 1 | Short title & commencement | Title includes state name; extends to whole State/UT; comes into force on date notified by State Government. | Temporal hook: effective date is state-notified, not Act date. | model |
| MTA-2 | § 2 | Definitions | Landlord, tenant, Rent Authority, property manager, rental agent. | Entity definitions for inventory. | model |
| MTA-3 | § 3 | Act not to apply | Exempts govt. premises, company housing, religious/charitable institutions, waqf, etc.; voluntary opt-in. | Determines whether MTA regime applies. | model |
| MTA-4 | § 4 | Tenancy agreement | No letting without written agreement; joint submission to Rent Authority within 2 months; unique ID issued within 7 working days. | Mandatory written agreement; deposit terms must be in agreement. | model |
| MTA-5 | § 5 | Period of tenancy | Valid for agreed period; enhanced rent on overstay (§ 23). | Tenancy term & overstay charges. | model |
| MTA-7 | § 7 | Restriction on sub-letting | No sub-let/assignment without supplementary agreement reported within 2 months. | Sub-letting as eviction ground. | model |
| MTA-8 | § 8 | Rent | Rent payable is agreed rent. | Baseline for deposit cap calculation (2/6 months). | model |
| MTA-9 | § 9 | Revision of rent | As per agreement; improvements with prior written consent. | Rent-increase disputes adjacent to deposit. | model |
| MTA-10 | § 10 | Dispute on rent revision | Rent Authority may determine revised rent. | Forum for rent disputes. | model |
| MTA-11 | § 11 | Security deposit | Cap: 2 months' rent (residential), 6 months' (non-residential); refund on date of handing over vacant possession after deducting tenant liability. | **Core provision** for deposit cap & refund timing. | model |
| MTA-20 | § 20 | Essential services | No withholding of essential supply; compensation up to 2 months' rent. | Landlord cannot coerce tenant by cutting services; supports deposit recovery. | model |
| MTA-21 | § 21 | Grounds for eviction | Non-payment of rent 2 consecutive months, unauthorised sub-letting, misuse, repair/reconstruction, structural changes. | Eviction grounds landlords cite to withhold deposit. | model |
| MTA-23 | § 23 | Enhanced rent for overstay | 2× rent for first 2 months, 4× thereafter. | Overstay deduction basis. | model |
| MTA-29 | § 29 | Notice by tenant | At least 1 month's written notice before giving up possession (unless agreement differs). | Notice compliance affects deposit refund. | model |
| MTA-30 | § 30 | Rent Authority | Appointed by District Collector/Magistrate (not below Deputy Collector). | First forum. | model |
| MTA-32 | § 32 | Appeal to Rent Court | Appeal within 30 days of Rent Authority order. | Appellate step. | model |
| MTA-33 | § 33 | Rent Court | Appointed by District Collector. | Second forum. | model |
| MTA-34 | § 34 | Rent Tribunal | State may appoint District/Additional District Judge. | Final forum. | model |
| MTA-35 | § 35 | Procedure | Natural justice; dispose within 60 days where possible; max 3 adjournments. | Timeliness expectation. | model |
| MTA-37 | § 37 | Appeal to Rent Tribunal | Appeal within 30 days of Rent Court order. | Appellate step. | model |
| MTA-40 | § 40 | Bar of civil court jurisdiction | No civil court shall entertain matters covered by Act. | Forum exclusivity. | model |
| MTA-44 | § 44 | Power to make rules | State Government may make rules. | Rule-making / temporal changes. | model |

## D. Cross-cutting provisions often litigated with deposit disputes

| ID | Law | Provision | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| CC-1 | Consumer Protection Act, 2019 | Housing/rental services as service | Tenants sometimes forum-shop via consumer fora; rent-control exclusivity (e.g., MTA § 40) may bar. |
| CC-2 | Indian Penal Code / Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita | Cheating, criminal breach of trust | Criminal route for deposit fraud; civil recovery preferred. |
| CC-3 | Limitation Act, 1963 | Art. 22 (money lent/advanced) / Art. 113 (residuary) | Limitation period for deposit recovery suits (typically 3 years). |
| CC-4 | Court Fees Act / state amendments | Ad valorem court fee | Affects cost-benefit of civil recovery. |
