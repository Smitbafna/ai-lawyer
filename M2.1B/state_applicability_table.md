# M2.1B — State Applicability Table: Tenancy & Security-Deposit Law (India)

**Scope:** For each State/Union Territory, the tenancy regime governing a `security_deposit_dispute`,
the applicable deposit cap (if any), the forum, and verification status.

**Companion files:** `provision_inventory.md` (provision IDs), `source_register.md` (source IDs),
`temporal_metadata.md` (effective-date handling).

**Research date:** 2026-11-06. All entries must be verified against official sources before use
(see verification flags in § 4 and `provision_inventory.md` § E).

**Regime legend:**
- **MTA-adopter** — State/UT enacted the Model Tenancy Act (or equivalent with 2/6-month cap).
- **TN/AP-model** — Tamil Nadu / Andhra Pradesh 2017 Acts (3-month cap, "save agreement to the contrary").
- **Rent-control (no statutory cap)** — Older rent-control act applies to covered premises; deposit contractual; forum = Rent Controller / Civil Court.
- **TPA-only** — No tenancy statute identified; Transfer of Property Act, 1882 + contract governs; Civil Court.

## 3. Applicability decision tree

```
Q1. Is the premises in an MTA-adopter State/UT?
    YES -> MTA regime (if tenancy agreement registered/submitted per § 4).
          - But check: is the premises exempt under MTA § 3 (govt, company housing, religious/charitable, waqf)?
            YES -> TPA/contract governs (MTA does not apply); deposit contractual.
            NO  -> MTA cap applies (2/6 months); forum Rent Authority/Court/Tribunal; civil court barred (§ 40).
    NO  -> Q2.

Q2. Is the premises in Tamil Nadu or Andhra Pradesh?
    YES -> TN/AP-model: 3-month cap, "save agreement to the contrary"; forum Rent Court/Tribunal.
    NO  -> Q3.

Q3. Is the premises in a rent-control State/UT AND within the act's coverage
    (urban area, rent below threshold, construction-date eligibility)?
    YES -> Rent-control act + TPA; deposit contractual; forum Rent Controller / Civil Court.
    NO (exempt premises, e.g., high rent in Delhi/Karnataka) -> TPA-only effectively; deposit contractual; Civil Court.

Q4. Otherwise (no tenancy statute) -> TPA + contract; Civil Court.
```

**Important caveat:** Even in MTA-adopter states, the MTA applies only where the state has *notified* commencement and the parties have executed a written agreement submitted to the Rent Authority (MTA §§ 1, 4). Pre-existing tenancies and informal (oral) arrangements fall back to TPA/contract. Always check the tenancy's *start date* against the state's notified MTA commencement (see `temporal_metadata.md`).

## 4. Verification flags (unresolved)

1. **Arunachal Pradesh** — Act confirmed (2022); § 11 deposit text unread. Treat cap as "not verified"; do not assert a cap.
2. **Telangana / West Bengal / Gujarat** — rent-statute deposit clauses not confirmed by official text; marked contractual/TPA. (SRC-04 "could not confirm".)
3. **Chandigarh** — extension of Assam Tenancy Act 2021 by S.O. 2265(E) (6 May 2026) contested (reported P&H HC order). Treat as contested; do not rely on 2/6-month cap without judicial resolution.
4. **Madhya Pradesh** — 2010 Bill not confirmed enacted; treat as no statutory cap.
5. **Nagaland / Manipur / Mizoram / Meghalaya** — existence of rent-control statutes unconfirmed; TPA fallback assumed.
6. **State rent thresholds** (e.g., Delhi ₹3,500; Karnataka ₹3,500/₹2,000) must be verified against current amendments (Karnataka Rent (Amendment) Bill, 2025 noted).
7. **City-level customary deposit levels** are not law and are quoted only as market practice (SRC-04).
