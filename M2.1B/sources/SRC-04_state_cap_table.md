# Cached excerpt: SRC-04 state statutory security-deposit caps (as at 12 Sep 2026)

Source: Mithun Srivastava, Security Deposit Rules in India State by State 2026.
URL: https://mithunsrivastava.com/security-deposit-rules-india-by-state/

States and UTs WITH a statutory cap:
- Assam: Assam Tenancy Act 2021, 2 months residential / 6 months non-residential
- Uttar Pradesh: UP Regulation of Urban Premises Tenancy Act 2021, 2 / 6
- Uttarakhand: Uttarakhand Tenancy Act 2021, 2 / 6
- Arunachal Pradesh: Arunachal Pradesh Tenancy Act 2022, not verified
- Andaman and Nicobar Islands: Tenancy Regulation 2023, 2 / 6
- Dadra and Nagar Haveli and Daman and Diu: Tenancy Regulation 2023, 2 / 6
- Lakshadweep: Tenancy Regulation 2023, 2 / 6
- Jammu and Kashmir: J and K Tenancy Act 2025, 2 / 6, assented 1 Nov 2025
- Tamil Nadu: TN Act 2017, 3 months, save agreement to the contrary
- Andhra Pradesh: AP Act 2017, 3 months, save agreement to the contrary
- Chandigarh: Assam Tenancy Act 2021 as extended by S O 2265 E 6 May 2026, 2 / 6, contested

NO statutory cap: Maharashtra, Delhi, Karnataka, Telangana, West Bengal, Kerala, Gujarat, Rajasthan,
Madhya Pradesh, Chhattisgarh, Bihar, Jharkhand, Odisha, Punjab, Haryana, Himachal Pradesh, Goa,
Puducherry, Sikkim, Nagaland, Manipur, Mizoram, Meghalaya, Tripura, Ladakh.
